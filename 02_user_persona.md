# User Persona

**Name:** Arjun  
**Age:** 20  
**Role:** College student / trainee

## Goals
- Keep all assignments in one place.
- Know what is due next.
- Complete tasks on time.
- Quickly understand assignment requirements.

## Frustrations
- Missing deadlines.
- Searching through messages for task instructions.
- Apps that have too many unnecessary controls.

## Preferred Experience
Simple screens, readable labels, clear due dates, and obvious actions.
